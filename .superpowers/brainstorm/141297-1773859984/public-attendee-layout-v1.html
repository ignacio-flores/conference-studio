Recommended Direction

  • Keep `Structure` as the landing tab.
  • Add a read-only session drawer with paper titles, authors, and abstracts.
  • Keep `Programme` as the second tab for the existing schedule flow.
  • Keep `Papers` as the third tab, but make it attendee-oriented instead of admin-oriented.

What To Remove

  • No inspector language.
  • No edit buttons, add-room controls, or empty-slot affordances.
  • No admin metrics like overflow or inactive sessions in the public view.
